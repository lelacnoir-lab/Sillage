New creative tools

EVOLVE

Add notes progressively within your scale. Choose octaves -1, 0 or +1 and set note length. Reset one track or all tracks to the original phrase.

Sound Design

Choose an engine in PRESET INIT, sculpt it in dedicated pages and finish with SAVE SOUND, a user slot and a name.

Live transpose

Hold ENV. Choose melodic tracks and transpose from the keyboard, immediately or on a timing grid. Pending keys flash; drums stay unchanged.

Quantized FX HOLD + FILL

Long-press FX for live effects. Quantize start and stop up to two bars. Hold E5 FILL to call your prepared conditional steps.

The Sillage foundation

Editable DRUM/DRUM-X kits, bar editing, automation and parameter locks, per-track SONG chains, groove, mutes, solos and Performance Loop. Four-band EQ per track, Juno Chorus I/II, shared reverb and delay buses, ping-pong delay and Final Master stereo width. Web editor with grouped controls, sound families and A/B comparison.

EVOLVE and live transpose are temporary and are not stored in projects. Fill conditions apply to a whole step, including simultaneous drum hits. The original 2.2-based reverb processing is retained. This FM-1 binary still displays “Sillage V3 beta 1” internally; it is unchanged from the compiled test candidate. Back up your music before installation.
